# Pathways status

[44](44_ensemble_prevented_fractions/SUMMARY.md) ran every arm on the four settings'
30-member ensembles from [43](../settings/43_delayed_epidemic_ensembles/SUMMARY.md), with
stisim's acquisition cofactors and ANC testing in the baseline. Every arm's prevented
fraction scaled with the Magic cure's across settings.

[45](45_barker_cofactors_anc_arms/SUMMARY.md) redrew the HPrev mature setting with
Barker 2022's acquisition cofactors for women in both sexes (`hiv_ng.rel_sus_hiv_ng` = 2.81,
`hiv_ct.rel_sus_hiv_ct` = 1.49, `hiv_tv.rel_sus_hiv_tv` = 1.54, `hiv_syph.rel_sus_hiv_syph`
= 1.67) and ANC HIV testing and syphilis screening off (`anc.start` = inf). Its 30 members
are in `45_barker_cofactors_anc_arms/outputs/ensemble.json`, regenerated by `run.py`. In
45 the Magic cure prevents a median 40.6% of SOC's HIV infections at 15-49 over 2027-2040,
the panel-based arms 3.6-12.4% (medians) and Magic PrEP 19.0% at 3-month courses and 38.3%
at 12-month courses. ANC testing switched on in 2027, with the panel at the ANC HIV test,
adds a median 0.6 percentage points to the panel.

Two switches in `src/sim.py` came with 45: `anc.start` (None from the run's start, a year,
or inf for never) and `sti_screening.anc_only` (the panel at the ANC HIV test alone, country
setups only).
