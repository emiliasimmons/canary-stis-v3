- [Canary study concept: originating note and May 2026 convening deck](../pages/canary-study-concept.md) -- The idea the project started from: the pathway decomposition proposed from STI diagnostics to HIV incidence, the counterfactual arm ladder meant to separate the pathways, and the empirical anchors named for it.

- [Implementation and applications of EMOD, an individual-based multi-disease modeling platform](../pages/emod-platform.md) -- Design and software-process description of IDM's EMOD agent-based platform, including how HIV inherits from a generic STI transmission layer and how STI cofactors are configured.
- [Virulence and pathogenesis of HIV-1 infection: an evolutionary perspective](../pages/fraser-2014-hiv-virulence-evolution.md) -- Review arguing viral genotype explains ~33% of set-point viral load variance, with SPVL sitting near the value that maximizes transmission potential.
- [Reductions in HIV incidence are likely to increase the importance of key population programmes for HIV control in sub-Saharan Africa](../pages/garnett-2021-key-populations.md) -- Commentary arguing that as HIV incidence falls, transmission concentrates in high-risk core groups, so acquisition-side risk factors misdirect prevention.

- [Behaviour change in generalised HIV epidemics: impact of reducing cross-generational sex and delaying age at sexual debut](../pages/hallett-2007-cross-generational-sex-debut.md) -- Age-structured deterministic HIV model showing that age-disparate mixing and later sexual debut move population-level risk far less than individual-level risk factor analyses imply.
- [Canonical figures in HIV transmission modeling: a taxonomy with named examples](../pages/hiv-modeling-figure-archetypes.md) -- A research agent's taxonomy of the figure archetypes HIV modeling papers reuse, each with its axes, encoding, and named exemplars, plus the field's plotting conventions.
- [Predicting HIV incidence in the SEARCH Trial using EMOD-HIV](../pages/jewell-2021-search-trial-emod-hiv.md) -- EMOD-HIV model of UTT in Uganda/Kenya; predicted 4-43% incidence reduction depending on counterfactual
- [New HIV Infections Among Key Populations and Their Partners in 2010 and 2022, by World Region](../pages/korenromp-2024-key-population-new-infections.md) -- UNAIDS multi-model estimates of the share of adult new HIV infections falling on sex workers, their clients, MSM, PWID, transgender women, and non-key-population partners, by region and year.
- [Modeling the Impact of Uganda's Safe Male Circumcision Program: Implications for Age and Regional Targeting](../pages/kripke-2016-uganda-smc-age-targeting.md) -- DMPPT 2.0 compartmental model of age- and region-targeted male circumcision scale-up in Uganda, giving circumcisions and cost per HIV infection averted.
- [Approximate Bayesian Computation and Simulation-Based Inference for Complex Stochastic Epidemic Models](../pages/mckinley-2018-abc-history-matching-epidemics.md) -- Review of ABC variants and a worked failure of ABC-SMC on a 22-parameter, 18-output individual-based HIV model in Uganda, against history matching on the same model.
- [Growing gender disparity in HIV infection in Africa: sources and policy implications](../pages/monod-2023-gender-disparity-hiv-rakai.md) -- RCCS incidence, viral suppression and deep-sequence transmission flows by age and sex over 2003-2018, plus counterfactual scenarios closing the male suppression gap.
- [Longitudinal population-level HIV epidemiologic and genomic surveillance highlights growing gender disparity of HIV transmission in Uganda](../pages/monod-2023-genomic-surveillance-rakai-natmicro.md) -- Fifteen years of RCCS incidence plus deep-sequence phylogenetics, giving age- and sex-specific incidence rates 2003-2018 and transmission flows showing men's contribution rising from 58% to 63%
- [Trends and country-level variation in age at first sex in sub-Saharan Africa among birth cohorts entering adulthood between 1985 and 2020](../pages/nguyen-2022-age-first-sex-ssa.md) -- Bias-adjusted Bayesian estimates of the full age-at-first-sex distribution by sex, country, and birth cohort across 42 sub-Saharan African countries.
- [PopART-IBM, a highly efficient stochastic individual-based simulation model of generalised HIV epidemics](../pages/pickles-2021-popart-ibm.md) -- Model description and ABC calibration of an individual-based HIV model to 248 age- and sex-stratified prevalence and cascade targets in a Zambian HPTN 071 community.
- [Age patterns of HIV incidence in eastern and southern Africa: a modelling analysis of observational population-based cohort studies](../pages/risher-2021-age-patterns-hiv-incidence-alpha.md) -- Bayesian reconstruction of age-specific HIV incidence 2000-17 in six ALPHA cohorts including Rakai, giving mean age at infection, age distribution of new infections, and birth-cohort lifetime risk.
- [Comparison of Empirically Derived and Model-Based Estimates of Key Population HIV Incidence and the Distribution of New Infections by Population Group in Sub-Saharan Africa](../pages/stevens-2024-key-population-incidence-ssa.md) -- Triangulation of empirical incidence-rate ratios, 94 transmission-model applications, and UNAIDS estimates; FSW incidence 8.6x and MSM 41.8x sex-matched total-population incidence, with 12% of 2021 SSA infections among FSW and MSM

- [Estimating the contribution of key populations towards HIV transmission in South Africa](../pages/stone-2021-key-populations-hiv-south-africa.md) -- Deterministic HIV model ("Key-Pop") splitting South African adults into low-risk, female sex workers, their clients and MSM, giving transmission population attributable fractions by partnership type and the impact of ART scale-up by group.
- [Voluntary Medical Male Circumcision for HIV Prevention in Swaziland: Modeling the Impact of Age Targeting](../pages/vmmc-age-targeting-swaziland.md) -- Compartmental model of VMMC scale-up by client age group in Swaziland, reporting infections averted, incidence reduction, and cost per infection averted.
- [Health impact, budget impact, and price threshold for cost-effectiveness of lenacapavir for HIV pre-exposure prophylaxis in eastern and southern Africa](../pages/wu-2024-lenacapavir-price-threshold.md) -- EMOD-HIV modelling of six-monthly injectable lenacapavir in South Africa, Zimbabwe and western Kenya.
