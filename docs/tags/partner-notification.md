### Entries (18)
- [Improving HIV Case Finding Through Index Testing: Findings from Health Facilities in 12 Districts of South Africa, October 2019-September 2021](../pages/aheron-2024-index-testing-south-africa.md) -- PEPFAR programme data on the HIV index-testing cascade in 371 South African facilities; 2.6% of tests but 17.8% positivity, 1.0 contacts elicited per client, 51% of contacts tested.
- [Partner notification for sexually transmitted infections in developing countries: a systematic review](../pages/alam-2010-partner-notification-developing-countries.md) -- Systematic review of 39 studies on STI partner notification in low- and middle-income countries; median 54% of partners notified, with spousal partners reached far more than casual ones.
- [Canary study concept: originating note and May 2026 convening deck](../pages/canary-study-concept.md) -- The idea the project started from: the pathway decomposition proposed from STI diagnostics to HIV incidence, the counterfactual arm ladder meant to separate the pathways, and the empirical anchors named for it.
- [Heterosexual HIV-1 transmission after initiation of antiretroviral therapy: a prospective cohort analysis](../pages/donnell-2010-art-hiv-transmission.md) -- Serodiscordant-couple cohort giving genetically linked HIV transmission rates per 100 person-years by ART status, CD4 stratum, and plasma viral load.
- [Assisted Partner Notification Services Are Safe and Effective as They Are Brought to Scale in Mozambique](../pages/golden-2023-aps-safety-mozambique.md) -- Prospective evaluation of APS at three Maputo facilities (2016-2019); 55% of tested partners newly HIV-positive, case-finding index 0.36, and a 1.2% adverse-event rate
- [Sexually Transmitted Infections among HIV-1-Discordant Couples](../pages/guthrie-2009-stis-hiv-discordant-couples.md) -- Enrollment cross-section of 416 Kenyan HIV-discordant couples: 16% of couples had a curable STI, and a partner's STI raised the odds 5.9-fold
- [Strategies to increase couples HIV testing and counselling in sub-Saharan Africa: a systematic review](../pages/hampanda-2023-couples-hiv-testing-strategies.md) -- Narrative systematic review of 29 studies categorising 10 strategies to promote couples HIV testing, with uptake spanning 2% to 87% depending on strategy and denominator
- [Scale-up of HIV index testing in an urban population: Experiences and achievements from Nairobi County, Kenya](../pages/joel-2022-index-testing-nairobi.md) -- Two-year scale-up of HIV index testing across 48 Nairobi County facilities; ~24-25% positivity among tested partners, index testing's share of new diagnoses rising from 7.5% to 28.6%
- [Scaling Up Testing for HIV Infection Among Contacts of Index Patients, 20 Countries, 2016-2018](../pages/lasry-2019-index-testing-20-countries.md) -- PEPFAR programme data on 1.7 million HIV index tests, giving positivity by age, sex and country and the yield advantage of index testing over all testing approaches combined.
- [Integration of antenatal syphilis screening in an urban HIV clinic: a feasibility study](../pages/manabe-antenatal-syphilis-integration.md) -- Kampala HIV antenatal cohort, 570 women, 5.1% RPR-positive but 1.8% treponemal-confirmed, and 34% of partners returned for treatment.
- [Prevalence and co-occurrence of sexually transmitted infections among heterosexual couples in southern Uganda](../pages/moffa-2026-sti-couples-stips.md) -- Population-based STIPS analysis of 423 cohabiting couples showing 37% had a curable STI, with strong within-couple and cross-pathogen clustering
- [Low male partner attendance after syphilis screening in pregnant women leads to worse birth outcomes: the Syphilis Treatment of Partners (STOP) randomised control trial](../pages/parkes-ratanshi-2020-stop-partner-notification-syphilis.md) -- Three-arm RCT of partner notification strategies for syphilis in pregnant women at Kampala ANCs; only 25% of male partners attended across all arms, with no significant difference between notification slips, SMS, or phone calls.
- [Viral Load and Heterosexual Transmission of HIV-1](../pages/quinn-2000-viral-load-heterosexual-transmission.md) -- Rakai discordant-couple cohort giving per-partnership HIV incidence by viral load, with null STI effects after adjustment.
- [HIV Partner Notification Values and Preferences Among Sex Workers, Fishermen, and Mainland Community Members in Rakai, Uganda: A Qualitative Study](../pages/quinn-2018-hiv-partner-notification-values-rakai.md) -- Focus groups and interviews in Rakai on the acceptability of passive, provider, and contract partner-notification approaches across key and general populations
- [Brief Report: Active HIV Case Finding in the City of Kigali, Rwanda: Assessment of Voluntary Assisted Partner Notification Modalities to Detect Undiagnosed HIV Infections](../pages/remera-2021-vapn-modalities-kigali.md) -- Pilot of three VAPN modalities across 23 Kigali facilities; all three comparable in yield, 7.1% partner positivity, higher for partners of male and newly-diagnosed index cases
- [Male-Partner Treatment to Prevent Recurrence of Bacterial Vaginosis (StepUp)](../pages/stepup-bv-partner-treatment.md) -- Randomized trial in Australian couples where treating the male partner cut 12-week bacterial vaginosis recurrence from 63% to 35%, the strongest partner-treatment effect size available.
- [Assisted HIV partner notification services in resource-limited settings: experiences and achievements from Cameroon](../pages/tih-2019-apns-cameroon.md) -- Nine years of programmatic assisted partner notification in Cameroon, giving yield per index case, cascade attrition, and social-harm rates.
- [Time to HIV testing of sexual contacts identified by HIV-positive index clients in Siaya County, Kenya](../pages/wekesa-2020-time-to-test-partner-services-siaya.md) -- Routine partner-services program data on 6,845 elicited sexual contacts; median 14.5 days from notification to HIV test, with only 48.8% projected to ever test over 1.25 years.
