- [FACTS-001: pericoital tenofovir 1% vaginal gel for HIV prevention in South African women](../pages/delany-moretlwe-2018-facts-001-tenofovir-gel.md) -- Phase 3 trial finding no overall efficacy for pericoital tenofovir gel, with protection only among women with detectable drug and an HSV-2 risk gradient.
- [Adolescent girls and young women: key populations for HIV epidemic control](../pages/dellar-2015-agyw-key-populations.md) -- Narrative review of why 15-24 year old women in southern Africa acquire HIV 5-7 years earlier than men, and what prevention has and has not worked.
- [Implementation and applications of EMOD, an individual-based multi-disease modeling platform](../pages/emod-platform.md) -- Design and software-process description of IDM's EMOD agent-based platform, including how HIV inherits from a generic STI transmission layer and how STI cofactors are configured.

- [Male circumcision for HIV prevention in men in Rakai, Uganda: a randomised trial](../pages/gray-2007-male-circumcision-rakai-trial.md) -- Randomised trial in Rakai showing 51% efficacy of adult male circumcision against HIV acquisition in men, with control-arm HIV incidence of 1.33 per 100 person-years and STI-symptom-stratified incidence.
- [Sexually Transmitted Infections among HIV-1-Discordant Couples](../pages/guthrie-2009-stis-hiv-discordant-couples.md) -- Enrollment cross-section of 416 Kenyan HIV-discordant couples: 16% of couples had a curable STI, and a partner's STI raised the odds 5.9-fold
- [Treatment of sexually transmitted infections for HIV prevention: end of the road or new beginning?](../pages/hayes-2010-sti-treatment-hiv-prevention.md) -- Review of nine RCTs and observational evidence for STI treatment as HIV prevention, with explanations for the divergent trial results.
- [Curable sexually transmitted infections among women with HIV in sub-Saharan Africa](../pages/jarolimova-2022-curable-stis-women-hiv-ssa.md) -- Meta-analysis of 67 studies giving pooled STI prevalence among women with HIV in SSA (gonorrhoea 3.5%, chlamydia 4%, trichomoniasis 15.6%) and elevated risk relative to HIV-negative women (RR 1.54-1.89).
- [To stem HIV in Africa, prevent transmission to young women](../pages/laga-2001-hiv-young-women-africa.md) -- AIDS editorial assembling the 15-24 sex differential in HIV prevalence across east and southern Africa and attributing it to age-disparate mixing, per-act susceptibility, and untreated STI.
- [Sexually transmitted infections and migration in Uganda: A population-based study](../pages/moffa-2025-sti-migration-uganda.md) -- STIPS cross-sectional prevalence of four curable STIs by recent migration status in two Rakai communities, adults 18-49, 2019.
- [Prevalence and co-occurrence of sexually transmitted infections among heterosexual couples in southern Uganda](../pages/moffa-2026-sti-couples-stips.md) -- Population-based STIPS analysis of 423 cohabiting couples showing 37% had a curable STI, with strong within-couple and cross-pathogen clustering
- [HIV susceptibility in women: The roles of genital inflammation, sexually transmitted infections and the genital microbiome](../pages/mtshali-2021-genital-inflammation-hiv-susceptibility.md) -- Narrative review of the mucosal mechanism behind the STI cofactor effect in women, reporting a 3-fold higher HIV acquisition risk with raised genital pro-inflammatory cytokines and arguing syndromic management misses the asymptomatic infections that drive it
- [Estimating per-act HIV transmission risk: a systematic review](../pages/patel-2014-per-act-hiv-transmission-risk.md) -- CDC synthesis of per-act HIV acquisition probabilities by exposure route, 8 per 10,000 receptive and 4 per 10,000 insertive penile-vaginal, plus relative risks for genital ulcer disease, viral load, stage, condoms, circumcision and ART
- [Viral Load and Heterosexual Transmission of HIV-1](../pages/quinn-2000-viral-load-heterosexual-transmission.md) -- Rakai discordant-couple cohort giving per-partnership HIV incidence by viral load, with null STI effects after adjustment.

- [Syphilis Diagnosis and Treatment: State of The Art](../pages/trovato-2021-syphilis-diagnosis-treatment.md) -- Dermatology-oriented narrative review of syphilis staging, test accuracy and penicillin regimens, giving per-stage durations, a 33% per-exposure transmission risk and sensitivity/specificity ranges for nine diagnostic assays
- [Age of coital debut and HIV seroprevalence among women in Durban](../pages/wand-2012-coital-debut-hiv-durban.md) -- MIRA screening and follow-up cohort in KwaZulu-Natal linking early sexual debut, lifetime partner count, HSV2 and curable STIs to HIV prevalence and incidence in women
