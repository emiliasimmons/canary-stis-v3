The prevention-targeting pathway routes a person with a positive point-of-care STI test
into PrEP, and the HIV infections it averts are the product of three fractions: whether
a positive test raises PrEP uptake, the effective coverage that uptake decays to, and the
drug's efficacy at that coverage. The first two fractions are small in Eastern and
Southern Africa, so the pathway is bounded well below the drug's trial efficacy. Effective
coverage in this region runs in the low tens of percent at best, and single digits by
pharmacological measure, and a positive STI test adds little to uptake beyond the clinic
contact itself, with one measured exception at the antenatal door that came at the cost of
worse continuation. A model that sets the prevention-targeting pathway near trial efficacy
contradicts the delivery evidence.

## PrEP efficacy under adherence

Oral PrEP reduces HIV acquisition by 54% across trials and by 73% at 70% adherence or
above ([Chou 2019](chou-2019-prep-systematic-review.md), RR 0.46 and 0.27), and the
per-act heterosexual estimate is a 71% reduction ([Patel
2014](patel-2014-per-act-hiv-transmission-risk.md), RR 0.29). Twice-yearly injectable
lenacapavir gave a 100% reduction in cisgender women under trial delivery ([Bekker
2024](bekker-2024-purpose1-lenacapavir.md), zero infections), the upper bound for
a long-acting product. Each figure is measured under trial-grade or adherence-conditioned
delivery, so each describes the drug in an adherent user. Delivered coverage is lower.

## Effective coverage as delivered

The two placebo-controlled trials of tenofovir PrEP in African women returned no effect
because the drug rarely reached the exposure. VOICE found no arm reduced acquisition, with
plasma tenofovir detected in a quarter to a third of samples ([Marrazzo
2015](marrazzo-2015-voice-prep-african-women.md)), and FEM-PrEP was null with fewer than
40% of active-arm controls showing recent-dose drug ([Van Damme
2012](van-damme-2012-fem-prep.md)). PURPOSE 1 reproduced this for daily oral F/TAF in
women aged 16-25, which showed no measurable population effect against background
incidence, again on adherence ([Bekker 2024](bekker-2024-purpose1-lenacapavir.md), IRR
0.84).

Demonstration and programme cohorts trace the cascade from offer to protective drug, and
these are the nearest to Rakai. In POWER across Kenya and South Africa, 94% of women
initiated PrEP, 31% returned for the one-month refill, and 20% persisted without
a substantial gap through six months, with 47% of a drug-level subsample at four or more
doses per week ([Celum 2022](celum-2022-power-prep-agyw.md)). In a Kampala AGYW cohort,
90% were willing and 30.6% took up PrEP, median time on drug was 2.5 months, and
protective plasma tenofovir appeared in 3 of 42 samples at months 1 to 3 and none of 58
thereafter ([Mayanja 2022](mayanja-2022-prep-uptake-agyw-kampala.md)). The SEARCH rollout
in rural Kenya and Uganda reached 27% uptake among elevated-risk adults, with
self-reported adherence falling from 40% at week 4 to 27% at week 72 ([Koss
2020](koss-2020-prep-uptake-adherence-search.md), from abstract). The Rakai-area
priority-population programme reached 92% uptake but a median retention of 45 days, with
the highest-risk female subgroups dropping out fastest ([Kagaayi
2021](kagaayi-2021-prep-uptake-south-central-uganda.md)). Directly observed dosing fixes
what those adherence bands mean: two to three doses per week gives an 84% risk reduction
and four or more gives near-complete protection ([Anderson
2017](anderson-2017-tfv-dp-dbs-directly-observed-therapy.md)), so a cohort mostly below
four doses per week has little protection.

## Uptake and adherence by risk level

Whether uptake and adherence track risk upward or downward decides whether targeting
recovers coverage, and the evidence splits by population. In VOICE, drug detection
concentrated in older, married, multiparous women, who were at lower HIV risk, so the
women who took the drug needed it least ([Marrazzo
2015](marrazzo-2015-voice-prep-african-women.md)). The Kampala AGYW cohort ran the other
way, with uptake rising steeply with reported partner count, adjusted RR 4.70 for ten or
more partners against one ([Mayanja 2022](mayanja-2022-prep-uptake-agyw-kampala.md)).
Perceived risk predicts initiation but not adherence in this age group ([Velloza
2022](velloza-2022-hiv-salience-scale-prep-kampala.md), from abstract, aRR 1.07 per point
for dispensing, null for adherence), and risk perception shifts over time, driving both
uptake and discontinuation as a woman reassesses ([Ngure
2021](ngure-2021-perceived-risk-prep-kenya.md)). Occupational mobility, which defines the
highest-risk groups here, is the dominant driver of discontinuation ([Ddaaki
2021](ddaaki-2021-prep-barriers-facilitators-uganda.md), from abstract), so attrition
concentrates in the same network positions a targeting arm most wants to hold. Uptake can
rise with partner count while retention falls fastest in the highest-risk groups, so
a single effective-coverage multiplier applied across all risk groups is not supported.

## Effect of a positive STI test on PrEP uptake

One entry measures the specific step the pathway assumes, and it is off-site. In a Cape
Town antenatal cohort, point-of-care STI testing doubled the odds of same-day PrEP
initiation against lab-based testing, 90% versus 78% ([de Voux
2023](voux-2022-poc-sti-prep-anc-cape-town.md), aOR 2.07), with same-day results and
treatment the proposed mechanism, 79% treated on the day against 0% in the lab arm. In the
same study, three-month continuation was lower in the POC group, 54% versus 64% (aOR
0.65), so the same-day prompt raised initiation but not sustained use. The gain is 12
percentage points of initiation at an antenatal screening door in pregnant urban women,
partly reversed at continuation. Against it, a prevalent STI at enrolment was not
associated with PrEP uptake in the Kampala AGYW cohort ([Mayanja
2022](mayanja-2022-prep-uptake-agyw-kampala.md), unadjusted RR 1.09). Stated-preference
work points the direction of the antenatal result, with STI-positive AGYW more interested
in injectable PrEP ([Pfau 2025](pfau-2025-long-acting-prep-preferences-esa.md)), though
the same review records women who rate their STI risk high preferring condoms over
injectables, and explicit risk compensation among sex workers. The step has one positive
estimate, one null, and a preference signal, none from a general-population
symptomatic-care-seeking door and none from Rakai.

## Test-to-prevention linkage in DREAMS

DREAMS is the largest real-world test of routing a positive HIV test into prevention, the
same cascade step one node upstream. Among the most engaged invitees, PrEP awareness ran
near 90% while ever-use was 10 to 20% and current use 1 to 10%, and condom-promotion
participation was around 20 to 45% ([Floyd
2022](floyd-2022-dreams-risk-behaviour-cascades.md)). The package produced no detectable
additional HIV-incidence effect over three years against an ongoing secular decline
([Birdthistle 2021](birdthistle-2021-dreams-hiv-incidence.md)). Any test-to-prevention
step should assume a knowledge-to-uptake conversion in the single-digit-to-20% range.

## Impact of narrower targeting

How much a targeting signal buys is limited by how few people it selects and how much
higher their incidence is. EMOD-HIV modelling of lenacapavir shows the trade: aiming
narrowly raises the affordable per-dose price but averts fewer infections, and population
coverage of 1.6 to 4.0% averts 12 to 18% of infections over ten years at 95% assumed
efficacy ([Wu 2024](wu-2024-lenacapavir-price-threshold.md)). In Rakai, the national
PrEP-eligibility tool selects 29% of adults and separates incidence about two-fold, 0.91
against 0.41 per 100 person-years ([Ssempijja
2022](ssempijja-2022-prep-eligibility-hiv-incidence-rakai.md), from abstract), so
a behavioural targeting rule is a weak filter and most incident infections arise outside
the flagged group. A positive STI test is a sharper marker of a high-transmission position
than self-reported behaviour, and this eligibility contrast bounds how much incidence any
general-population targeting rule concentrates here.

## The PrEP cascade in Eastern and Southern Africa

| Step | Value in ESA / Uganda | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Drug efficacy, adherent | 73% (oral), ~100% (LEN) | [Chou 2019](chou-2019-prep-systematic-review.md), [Bekker 2024](bekker-2024-purpose1-lenacapavir.md) |
| STI-positive test raises uptake | +12 pts (ANC) to null (AGYW) | [de Voux 2023](voux-2022-poc-sti-prep-anc-cape-town.md), [Mayanja 2022](mayanja-2022-prep-uptake-agyw-kampala.md) |
| Uptake if offered | 27-31%, higher in demonstration cohorts | [Koss 2020](koss-2020-prep-uptake-adherence-search.md), [Mayanja 2022](mayanja-2022-prep-uptake-agyw-kampala.md) |
| 6-month persistence | ~20%, median retention 45 days | [Celum 2022](celum-2022-power-prep-agyw.md), [Kagaayi 2021](kagaayi-2021-prep-uptake-south-central-uganda.md) |
| Protective drug level | single digits (plasma) to ~47% (≥4 doses/wk subsample) | [Mayanja 2022](mayanja-2022-prep-uptake-agyw-kampala.md), [Celum 2022](celum-2022-power-prep-agyw.md) |

The steps multiply, so even the demonstration-cohort figures, which sit above routine
delivery, compose to effective protective coverage in the low tens of percent, and the
routine-programme retention of 45 days leaves most of the year uncovered.

## Parameterising the prevention-targeting pathway

The prevention-targeting pathway should be parameterised near these delivered numbers.
Trial efficacy overstates it. A positive STI test should route to sustained
pharmacological PrEP protection at a low rate that decays over months. Effective coverage
should be allowed to correlate with network position rather than applied uniformly, since
uptake can rise with partner count while retention falls fastest in the highest-risk
groups, and the current evidence does not fix the sign of the net effect on infections
averted. A run that returns a large HIV-incidence reduction through PrEP channelling at
symptomatic-care-seeking coverage should be checked against this cascade before it is
reported.

## Open questions

No entry measures the quantity the model most needs: the fraction of people who, presented
with a positive etiological STI result at a general-population symptomatic-care-seeking
door in Rakai, initiate and sustain PrEP to a protective level. The one direct
STI-test-to-PrEP estimate is antenatal, urban, South African, and confined to initiation
([de Voux 2023](voux-2022-poc-sti-prep-anc-cape-town.md)). The one general-risk Ugandan
cohort found no association of prevalent STI with uptake ([Mayanja
2022](mayanja-2022-prep-uptake-agyw-kampala.md)). Whether the STI-positive route
outperforms behavioural eligibility as a PrEP-targeting signal in the RCCS population is
unmeasured. Background PrEP coverage in Rakai is low and known only in slices: 0.5% inland
and 7.2% in a fishing community among HIV-negative adults in 2019 ([Grabowski
2022](grabowski-2022-stips-sti-burden-rakai.md)), and 5.7% current use among men with
sex-worker partners ([Feng 2025](feng-2025-fbsw-male-partners-rakai.md)).
