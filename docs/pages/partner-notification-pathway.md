Both arms of the model act at the symptomatic care-seeking door and add no partner tracing. The POC arm replaces syndromic management with an etiological panel, and neither arm follows a diagnosed person's partners. The evidence sets what that omission costs. Partner notification finds infected contacts at four to fourteen times general-testing positivity, and reaches roughly half of the contacts named. Once the cascade is composed, a diagnosed index case yields on the order of 0.1 to 0.4 newly diagnosed contacts. The concentration is the same targeting the POC arm already gets from symptomatic presentation, and the within-partnership reinfection that partner treatment interrupts is already carried by the shared sexual network, so a tracing arm would add case-finding among partners who never present and little beyond that.

## Yield concentration

In every programme that measured it, a named contact of a diagnosed person was more likely to be infected than someone reached by routine testing. In South African PEPFAR facilities, HIV positivity among tested contacts was 17.8% against 4.1% for all non-index modalities, a 4.3-fold difference, while index testing made up 2.6% of tests ([Aheron 2024](aheron-2024-index-testing-south-africa.md)). Nairobi County index testing ran 24.3% against 1.3% for other modalities, and its share of new diagnoses rose from 7.5% to 28.6% over two years ([Joel 2022](joel-2022-index-testing-nairobi.md)). Across 20 PEPFAR countries adult index positivity was 9.8% against 4.1% for all approaches combined ([Lasry 2019](lasry-2019-index-testing-20-countries.md)). Assisted partner services in Maputo diagnosed 55% of tested partners positive ([Golden 2023](golden-2023-aps-safety-mozambique.md)), and the Cameroon programme ran partner positivity at 51.8%, fourteen times national adult prevalence ([Tih 2019](tih-2019-apns-cameroon.md)).

The concentration weakens where HIV services are already dense. Uganda's index positivity in the 20-country data was 4.2%, below the adult average, which the authors attribute to high existing coverage ([Lasry 2019](lasry-2019-index-testing-20-countries.md)). Rakai is a high-coverage setting, so the yield a tracing arm would carry there sits at the low end of this range.

## Cascade reach

The cascade loses contacts at every step between naming and treatment, and composing the steps is what bounds a tracing arm.

| Step | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Contacts elicited per index | 1.0 (0.4 women, 1.1 men) | [Aheron 2024](aheron-2024-index-testing-south-africa.md) |
| Contacts named per index | 1.1 (range 1.1-1.3 over nine years) | [Tih 2019](tih-2019-apns-cameroon.md) |
| Partners notified | median 54% (STI, LMIC) | [Alam 2010](alam-2010-partner-notification-developing-countries.md) |
| Named contacts ever tested | 48.8% projected, median 14.5 days | [Wekesa 2020](wekesa-2020-time-to-test-partner-services-siaya.md) |
| Notified contacts tested | 71% | [Tih 2019](tih-2019-apns-cameroon.md) |
| New diagnoses per index client | ~0.09 | [Aheron 2024](aheron-2024-index-testing-south-africa.md) |
| Case-finding index (positives per index) | 0.36 | [Golden 2023](golden-2023-aps-safety-mozambique.md) |

Two steps carry most of the loss. About half of named contacts ever reach a testing door, at a median of two weeks rather than same-day ([Wekesa 2020](wekesa-2020-time-to-test-partner-services-siaya.md)). Of those tested in a mature programme, only a fifth were newly diagnosed rather than already known positive, so case-finding yield and new-diagnosis yield differ by about a factor of two ([Tih 2019](tih-2019-apns-cameroon.md)). Modality choice does not change the yield: three referral approaches in Kigali were indistinguishable ([Remera 2021](remera-2021-vapn-modalities-kigali.md)), and notification slips, SMS, and phone calls all reached about 25% male-partner attendance in the STOP trial ([Parkes-Ratanshi 2020](parkes-ratanshi-2020-stop-partner-notification-syphilis.md)). The one delivery mode that raised partner treatment was patient-delivered partner medication, at 74% of partners treated against 34% under referral, and it works by bypassing the testing step ([Alam 2010](alam-2010-partner-notification-developing-countries.md)).

## Partner treatment for the curable STIs

Notification of an asymptomatic partner performs worse for the curable STIs than the HIV cascade above. In a Kampala antenatal cohort, 58% of syphilis-positive women agreed to notify a partner and 34.5% of partners returned for treatment ([Manabe 2015](manabe-antenatal-syphilis-integration.md)). The STOP trial's 25% attendance is the syphilis figure under active outreach ([Parkes-Ratanshi 2020](parkes-ratanshi-2020-stop-partner-notification-syphilis.md)).

Partner treatment produced its largest measured effect in a bacterial-vaginosis trial, where treating the male partner cut female recurrence from 63% to 35% within 12 weeks ([StepUp](stepup-bv-partner-treatment.md), hazard ratio 0.37). Bacterial vaginosis is outside the model and the trial enrolled monogamous couples, which is the opposite of the risk structure the model's transmission rests on, so the number does not transfer as a parameter. The mechanism it demonstrates is that the untreated partner sustains reinfection, so clearing him removes a large part of what otherwise reads as treatment failure.

## Couple STI concordance

Couple data from the calibration setting size the pool a tracing arm would work over. Among Rakai cohabiting couples 37% had at least one curable STI, with strong within-couple concordance (gonorrhoea prevalence ratio 17.8 in partners of infected women) and cross-pathogen clustering ([Moffa 2026](moffa-2026-sti-couples-stips.md)). In Kenyan discordant couples a partner's curable STI raised the odds 5.9-fold, and 83% of affected couples were STI-discordant rather than concordant ([Guthrie 2009](guthrie-2009-stis-hiv-discordant-couples.md)). The discordant couples are the population a notification pathway exists to reach, and the concordant reinfection is what the shared-network model already represents through partnership edges. A referral-identified partner is a genuine transmission partner: nine of ten male-female referral pairs with sequence data in Lilongwe were phylogenetically linked ([Bell 2025](bell-2025-ahi-clustering-lilongwe.md)).

## Safety and social harm

Notification is sometimes withheld over intimate-partner-violence risk, and programme data put that cost low. Assisted partner services in Maputo ran a 1.2% adverse-event rate among index cases who notified, with events tied to fear of losing financial support ([Golden 2023](golden-2023-aps-safety-mozambique.md)). Nine years in Cameroon gave 6.3% reporting any adverse outcome, almost all partnership dissolution, and 1.1% physical intimate-partner violence after diagnosis ([Tih 2019](tih-2019-apns-cameroon.md)). The gendered loss sits upstream at elicitation, where South African providers withheld index testing from women screening positive for IPV risk, part of why female clients named 0.4 contacts against 1.1 for men ([Aheron 2024](aheron-2024-index-testing-south-africa.md)). These harm rates are low enough that safety does not decide whether to include the pathway.

## Acceptability in Rakai

Acceptable notification methods in Rakai split by partnership type. Passive referral, where the index person tells the partner, was preferred for primary partners across fishing and mainland communities, while provider referral was acceptable for reaching multiple casual partners, the partnerships that carry onward transmission. Providers named limited time, resources, and training as the constraint on any provider-assisted approach ([Quinn 2018](quinn-2018-hiv-partner-notification-values-rakai.md)). A tracing arm modelled at high provider-driven reach for casual partners would run against that constraint.

## Grounds for omitting the pathway

The pathway's two sources of value are both already represented in the model. The yield concentration, a named contact carrying four to fourteen times background positivity, is the same targeting the POC arm gets by acting at the symptomatic care-seeking door, where a person presenting with a syndrome is likewise enriched for infection. The within-partnership reinfection that partner treatment interrupts is carried by the shared risk-structured network, where an untreated partner re-exposes a treated one along an existing edge. What a tracing arm adds beyond these is case-finding among partners who never present, and the cascade figures give that increment as 0.1 to 0.4 diagnoses per index, with about half of named contacts never reaching a testing door, at a median two-week delay. Adding it would require a new elicitation-notification-testing-treatment cascade carrying its own coverage parameters. The omission is a scope decision, and this evidence is what bounds it.

## Open questions

The cascade fractions above come from HIV index testing and syphilis notification. No source measures partner tracing for gonorrhoea, chlamydia, or trichomoniasis in a general population, where index cases are more often symptomatic men than screen-detected women, so the reach of a tracing arm for the model's three discharge-causing pathogens is unmeasured. Patient-delivered partner medication is the delivery mode with the strongest measured effect, treating 74% of partners against 34% under referral ([Alam 2010](alam-2010-partner-notification-developing-countries.md)), and would be the form to represent if the omission is revisited.
