Source: [wawer-2011](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(10)61967-8)

Secondary analysis of two parallel randomised trials of adult male circumcision in Rakai,
Uganda, enrolling from September 2003 and following female partners to December 2007. Men
aged 15-49 and HIV-negative were randomised to immediate circumcision or circumcision after
24 months; their HIV-negative female partners (648 intervention, 597 control at analysis)
gave self-collected vaginal swabs at baseline, 12 and 24 months, genotyped for 14 high-risk
HPV types on the Roche Linear Array.

The headline result: at 24 months, high-risk HPV prevalence was 151/544 (27.8%) in partners
of the immediate-circumcision group against 189/488 (38.7%) in controls, prevalence risk
ratio 0.72 (95% CI 0.60-0.85). Incidence over the full two years was 20.7 vs 26.9 per 100
person-years, incidence rate ratio 0.77 (0.63-0.93), and 0.77 (0.65-0.92) after adjustment
for age, condom use, alcohol with sex, and partner number. Clearance of pre-existing
genotype-specific high-risk infections was 66% (376/568 genotype-infections) vs 59%
(339/573), risk ratio 1.12 (1.02-1.22).

## Relevance to project

HPV is outside the model's pathogen set (HIV plus gonorrhoea, chlamydia, syphilis,
trichomoniasis), so the efficacy result is context, not a target. What the paper supplies
directly is baseline description of the Rakai calibration population, from Table 1, for
1245 HIV-negative women and 1159 HIV-negative men in stable partnerships enrolled 2003-2006.

Self-reported STI symptoms in the previous year set a scale for the symptomatic care-seeking
door the SOC and POC arms share. Among women, 46-47% reported vaginal discharge, 19-21%
dysuria, and 13-14% genital ulcer disease. Among men the same three were 4%, 6-7%, and 9%.
The ten-fold sex difference in reported discharge is the quantity that matters for a
syndromic-management arm, since discharge is the syndrome that triggers presumptive
treatment, and it is recalled over a year rather than observed at a visit.

Partnership structure gives loose bounds for the risk-structured network, in a population
selected to be married or in long-term consensual unions: 96% of women reported one partner
in the previous year against 54-57% of men, 15-18% of women were in polygynous unions,
1-2% of women and 16-17% of men reported a non-marital relationship, and under 1% of either
sex reported transactional sex. Mean female partners per married man was 1.06-1.08. Condom
use was near-absent, with 81-85% of women and 64-67% of men reporting none in the previous
year.

The circumcision effect itself bears on the model only as a reminder that male circumcision
coverage rose across Rakai over the calibration window and independently lowers HSV-2,
syphilis, genital ulcer disease, and (in female partners) trichomoniasis. If the model
treats circumcision as a fixed background, that is an approximation this trial argues
against for trichomoniasis in particular.

## Limitations

The enrolled couples are a deliberately low-risk slice: HIV-negative at baseline, in stable
partnerships, compliant with a trial protocol. The authors say directly that the findings
may not transfer to people with HIV or to women with several partners. The behavioural and
symptom distributions above therefore describe married HIV-negative adults in Rakai, not the
general 15-49 population the model represents, and they will understate partner change and
transactional sex.

About 20% of women in each group had no enrolment swab because of a swab and transport-media
stock outage, cutting sample size and power. Follow-up was annual, so infections acquired
and cleared inside a year were missed and clearance times are not estimable. Newly detected
HPV mixes true acquisition, sampling variability, and reactivation of latent infection below
assay detection. Symptoms are self-reported over a one-year recall with no laboratory
confirmation, which is the main caution on using the discharge and ulcer figures.

The HPV endpoint had no pre-specified protocol of its own; it was specified in an NIH grant
proposal rather than the trial protocols, which set out the STI endpoints generally.
