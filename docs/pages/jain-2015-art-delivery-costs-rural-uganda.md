Micro-costing study nested in the EARLI trial (NCT01479634), which put 197 asymptomatic adults with CD4 above 350 cells/uL on ART at Bwizibwera Health Center, Mbarara, southwestern Uganda, from 2011. Combines administrative cost records, ART prices and a four-week time-and-motion study of staff activity, then projects six scale-up scenarios of increasing efficiency. Costs in 2012 US dollars, per person per year. Extracted content in [raw/10.1371/journal.pone.0143433/](../raw/10.1371/journal.pone.0143433/).

## Key points

- Observed cost in year 1 was US$987 per person-year, inflated by trial start-up and one-off baseline tests. At steady state after full enrolment it was US$628 with twice-yearly viral load testing and US$544 without.
- Substituting Makerere Joint AIDS Program salaries and Uganda's national two-tests-per-year laboratory schedule (CBC plus CD4) brought it to US$529 / US$445 (with / without viral load). Lowest available generic ARV prices took it to US$480 / US$396, Ministry of Health salary scales to US$405 / US$321, full use of the observed workday to US$334 / US$250, and a full 8-hour day to US$320 / US$236.
- Composition of the US$320 maximally efficient figure: US$39 personnel, US$106 ART, US$130 laboratory with viral load (US$46 without), US$46 administration and overhead. ART drug cost was 18-33% of total across the scenarios.
- Unit prices: viral load (plasma HIV-1 RNA) US$42 per test, CD4/CD8 count US$8.50, complete blood count US$4.50, Xpert TB test US$20.40, creatinine US$9.00, liver function US$22.50, electrolytes US$16.50, urine pregnancy US$3.00.
- Monthly ARV prices per patient: tenofovir/emtricitabine US$5.74, efavirenz US$7.20 in-study against US$3.07 at the MSF best price, lopinavir/ritonavir US$49.00 against US$19.64.
- Monthly salaries, three scales. Physician US$2,455 (trial) / US$1,083 (MJAP) / US$1,000 (Ministry of Health). Nurse US$784 / US$602 / US$240.80, with pharmacy and laboratory technicians on the same three figures as the nurse.
- Efficiency gains came almost entirely from patient throughput. Filling idle time in the observed workday raised patients served per year from 246 to 488, and moving to a full 8-hour day raised it to 602.
- Clinical context for the cost: 95.9% undetectable viral load at week 24, self-reported adherence 98% by 3-day recall, 8 hospitalisations and 2 deaths among 197 participants in year 1. Median CD4 at initiation 564 cells/uL (IQR 451-716), median age 35, 65% female, 70% in agriculture.

## Relevance to project

The unit-cost reference for HIV treatment on the Ugandan side of any costing this project does, and the closest available to Rakai in setting and period. The itemised laboratory prices matter most: US$42 per viral load and US$8.50 per CD4 count set the scale against which a point-of-care STI panel's per-test cost has to be judged, and they come from the same kind of rural Ugandan health centre the POC arm would be deployed in.

The salary table gives three defensible personnel cost levels for the nurse time a test-and-treat visit consumes, spanning a factor of three between research-study and Ministry of Health scales. If the POC arm is costed at all, that spread is a larger source of variation than the test kit price.

Nothing here bears on transmission, incidence, or STI epidemiology. The population is people already diagnosed and in care, the outcome is cost per person-year of delivery, and there is no comparator arm and no health outcome beyond viral suppression.

## Limitations

- One clinic, one rural district, and a research-supported cohort of asymptomatic high-CD4 patients selected for proximity (within 30 km) and absence of WHO stage 3/4 illness. Costs in an urban clinic or a sicker population are untested.
- Hospitalisation costs were excluded, on the grounds that events were rare (8/197 in year 1). Patient-side costs (transport, lost work time) and the cost of testing and recruiting new patients were also excluded.
- Models D through F depend on reassigning observed idle staff time to patient care, which is an accounting exercise rather than an observed outcome. The 75% and 50% efficiency sensitivity analyses raise Model E from US$320 to US$331 and US$345.
- Tenofovir/emtricitabine was donated to the trial; its price was imputed from the MSF 2014 database using tenofovir/lamivudine as an equivalent.
- 2012 dollars, 2014 drug prices. Generic ARV prices have moved considerably since, so the drug component is the least transportable line.
