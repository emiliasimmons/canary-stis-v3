The project's third pathway assumes a positive point-of-care STI test flags someone
sitting in a high-transmission position, so that treating or prevention-linking that
person averts more onward HIV infection than reaching an average case would. Two
conditions have to hold for the pathway to pay. Transmission must concentrate on positions
a diagnostic contact can identify, and reaching those positions must avert
disproportionate onward transmission. The Rakai evidence supports the first weakly and the
second only modestly. The payoff is larger in a spatially concentrated epidemic like rural
KwaZulu-Natal than in Rakai's fragmented one, so its size depends on the setting and is
not a fixed property of STI-guided targeting.

## Concentration of transmission on individual attributes

Transmission concentrates on individual attributes. In an agent-based transmission-chain
analysis a transmission-enhancing STI was present in 5.3% of non-transmitters against 57%
of agents causing five or more onward infections, a tenfold enrichment, with concurrency
the dominant driver ([Bershteyn 2013](bershteyn-2013-emod-hiv-age-partnering.md)). That
model also carries the constraint the pathway depends on: risk assortativity does not
emerge from heterogeneous risk in a network and must be written into the pair-formation
rule, so STI positivity marks a network position only when the model puts STI status and
partner choice on the same axis.

At the Rakai site the elevated-incidence positions are visible in observational data. HIV
incidence among female bar and restaurant workers ran 2.88 times that of women in
agriculture, and male transport workers 2.75 times men in agriculture, in the
mature-intervention period ([Popoola
2022](popoola-2022-untreated-hiv-occupational-groups-rakai.md)). Men reporting a female
bar or sex-worker partner had incidence 1.93 against 0.44 per 100 person-years
(age-adjusted IRR 4.46), and 77% of them also had a non-sex-worker partner, so the client
role bridges into the general population ([Feng
2025](feng-2025-fbsw-male-partners-rakai.md)). A nested case-control study made FSW
partnership the strongest single predictor of male incident HIV (OR 15.5), with 34% of
male infections attributed to sex with FSWs ([Bell
2026](bell-2026-hiv-transmission-declining-epidemic.md)). Infectiousness tracks the same
strata: persistent high-level viraemia reached 32.0% of visit-pairs among men aged 15-29
against 15.2% among women the same age, concentrated in those reporting more than three
partners, transactional sex, and fishing-community residence ([Rosen
2024](rosen-2024-durable-viral-suppression-rakai.md), [Grabowski
2021](grabowski-2021-persistent-viremia-utt-rakai.md)). STI positivity, network position,
and infectiousness co-locate in the same people.

## Transmission structure and flow at the Rakai site

The elevated-incidence positions in Rakai do not form a compact structure that
a diagnostic contact could find and interrupt. Transmission there is fragmented. Incident
cases rarely shared partners or venues, phylogenetic analysis found few large clusters,
and the FSW venues driving male incidence were mostly small, informal, and absent from
key-population program lists ([Bell
2026](bell-2026-hiv-transmission-declining-epidemic.md)). The pattern recurs regionally.
An STI-clinic study in Lilongwe found no phylogenetic cluster larger than two people
([Bell 2025](bell-2025-ahi-clustering-lilongwe.md)), and deep-sequence source attribution
in Zambia found high-risk age, out-of-community residence, and drug resistance
co-occurring at about the rates expected under independence, with 86.8% of transmissions
within-community, so no small combined-risk group carried the epidemic ([Hall
2024](hall-2024-hiv-transmission-sources-zambia.md)).

The high-prevalence communities also fail the second condition, that reaching them averts
transmission elsewhere. Rakai's Lake Victoria fishing hotspots receive more HIV than they
export. Scaled to the populations, an estimated 4.3% of transmissions ran inland to
lakeside against 1.7% the reverse, and 88.7% stayed within the inland communities
([Ratmann 2020](ratmann-2020-transmission-flow-hotspots-rakai.md)). Out-migrants from the
fishing sub-districts accounted for at most 1.3% of HIV cases in any inland sub-district
([Grabowski 2020](grabowski-2020-migration-hotspots-rakai.md)), the phylogeography shows
virus flowing into the fishing communities ([Bbosa
2019](bbosa-2019-fishing-community-phylogeography.md)), and
general-population-to-fisherfolk flow ran 1.56 times the reverse ([Bbosa
2020](bbosa-2020-directed-transmission-deep-sequences.md)). Much Rakai transmission
arrives from outside the community: 62% of people infected by an extra-household partner
were infected by a partner living outside their community, and 21% of infections had no
identifiable within-cohort source ([Grabowski
2014](grabowski-2014-viral-introductions-rakai.md)). Targeting a geographic or
occupational hotspot in this setting interrupts chains that largely stay inside it or
arrive from beyond the model's boundary.

## Concentration in other settings

Other settings show the compact core the Rakai data lack. In rural KwaZulu-Natal a single
peri-urban zone holding 7.5% of the area and 32% of the sampled population carried 40.8%
of HIV-positive people, 72.6% of phylogenetic links touched it, and mean node degree
inside was four times that outside ([Cuadros
2022](cuadros-2022-high-risk-geographies-kzn.md)). That paper draws the contrast directly:
the Rakai fishing-community work found under 11% of links crossing between high- and
low-prevalence communities against its own 72.6%, a gap it attributes to measuring
a contact network inside one community against a migration network across forty. The KZN
concentration does not transfer to a Rakai-parameterised model. Botswana and Kenya
phylogenetics sit between, with strong within-risk-group assortativity (88.5% of Kenyan
clusters within one risk group) alongside weak evidence that older men are a major source
and limited sex-work contribution ([Magosi 2022](magosi-2022-ya-tsie-phylogenetics.md),
[Nduva 2022](nduva-2022-hiv-flow-risk-groups-kenya.md)). The compact-core pattern belongs
to KwaZulu-Natal, and the Rakai flow studies do not reproduce it.

## Acquisition risk, transmission risk, and reported partnerships

Two measurement distinctions decide how a positive STI test should be read as a targeting
signal. The first is that acquisition risk is not transmission risk. Measuring who
acquires HIV overstates acquisition risk and understates transmission risk, so the value
of reaching a position is its transmission population attributable fraction, the share of
infections that disappears when that position's transmission is removed over a horizon,
computed by running the model with and without it ([Garnett
2021](garnett-2021-key-populations.md)). A cross-sectional share of new infections
understates the prevention value of a high-transmission position, because it omits the
onward transmission that reaching the position averts ([Stevens
2024](stevens-2024-key-population-incidence-ssa.md)). Both point to a counterfactual
comparison over time, which is the SOC-versus-POC design this project runs.

The second distinction is that the positions that transmit are not the positions
self-report identifies. In Rakai the median age gap between an incident infection in
a woman 15-24 and her transmitting partner was 9.0 years, against a reported partner age
gap of 3.6 years for the same women, so transmitting partners are drawn from an older tail
than the reported mixing matrix ([Monod
2023](monod-2023-genomic-surveillance-rakai-natmicro.md)). A laboratory STI result is
a current biomarker of activity, and it can resolve those transmitting positions better
than the self-reported behaviour prevention programmes usually screen on: removing
lab-diagnosed STI from an HIV risk score dropped its discrimination by 1 to 8 AUC points
([Jia 2022](jia-2022-hiv-risk-scores-ssa.md)).

## Size of the targeting payoff

The onward-transmission payoff of reaching one high-position individual is bounded, and it
grows as the epidemic matures. As incidence falls, transmission re-concentrates in
hard-to-reach core groups, and coverage gains land on people who are not the
high-frequency transmitters, because the social networks carrying health information do
not match the sexual networks carrying transmission ([Ortblad
2019](ortblad-2019-arc-of-hiv-epidemics.md), [Garnett
2021](garnett-2021-key-populations.md)). Narrow targeting shows steep diminishing returns.
Modelled lenacapavir delivered to female sex workers alone averted 6.0% of infections
while sustaining a high affordable per-dose price, and opening delivery to broader risk
strata raised infections averted to 42.5% at a fraction of that price ([Wu
2024](wu-2024-lenacapavir-price-threshold.md)). Scaling ART to clients of sex workers was
2.3 times more efficient per person-year than to low-risk adults but delivered near-equal
total impact, because the targeted group is small ([Stone
2021](stone-2021-key-populations-hiv-south-africa.md)). In a generalized epidemic where
roughly three quarters of new infections fall outside key populations ([Korenromp
2024](korenromp-2024-key-population-new-infections.md)), the ceiling on what any targeting
signal can capture is low. For the model, the third pathway should carry a modest prior in
a mature generalized epidemic like Rakai's, larger than in a homogeneous population and
smaller than in a spatially concentrated one, and be reported as a counterfactual
transmission fraction over a long horizon.

## Open questions

The fragmentation and sink findings rest on incomplete sampling. Rakai deep-sequence
studies place only about a third of a sequenced person's transmitters in the sampled data,
and sequencing excludes the treated and undersamples the mobile ([Ratmann
2019](ratmann-2019-deep-sequence-transmission-rakai.md), [Ratmann
2020](ratmann-2020-transmission-flow-hotspots-rakai.md)). Few observed clusters therefore
set a floor on connectedness and cannot exclude a core that sampling missed, and the
undersampling biases toward apparent fragmentation. No entry measures the onward
transmission averted by reaching an STI-positive person, so the pathway's magnitude is
a model output the canon can bound but not observe. It depends jointly on this synthesis
and on the STI-HIV cofactor evidence, since a targeting benefit routed through a positive
test also requires the cofactor to be non-trivial.
